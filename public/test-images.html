📋 How to Use These Test Images

1 Download or Print: Save the images above or print them on paper
2 Display Method:
  • Display on another phone/tablet screen
  • Print on white paper (recommended)
  • Show on computer monitor
3 Lighting: Ensure good, even lighting on the test image (avoid shadows)
4 Distance: Hold your camera 6-12 inches (15-30 cm) from the image
5 Stability: Keep the image steady and the camera stable for 2-3 seconds
6 Recognition: Look for the success message and 3D model appearing
7 Switch Models: Point at the other image to change 3D models
